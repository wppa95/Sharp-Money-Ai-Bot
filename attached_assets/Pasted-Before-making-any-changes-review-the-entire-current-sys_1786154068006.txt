🔴 PRIORITY #9 — LINE MOVEMENT CALCULATION

We found an alert that said the market moved DOWN while the displayed line went UP.

Movement must be calculated strictly from:

Previous line
→ Current line

Current: 18.5
Move: +5.0

Therefore:

Movement = UP

Do NOT confuse:

* Historical average
* Historical percentage
* Previous market line
* Current market line
* Line movement

Historical average is NOT the previous market line.
