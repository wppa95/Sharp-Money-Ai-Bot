2. TIER 1 DELIVERY RULE

🔴 TIER 1 = EVERY SUPPORTED SPORT EXCEPT NBA, MLB, NFL

For Tier 1 sports, everything is allowed as long as the prop has:

1. Valid direction

The model must have a real:

* OVER
* or UNDER

direction.

No direction = ❌ no Telegram.

2. Strong analysis

The existing analysis/qualification system must determine that the prop is a legitimate actionable pick.

Do NOT impose a separate numerical BQ or MQ hard gate on Tier 1.

IMPORTANT

For Tier 1:

BQ and MQ are NOT standalone blockers.

A Tier 1 prop can still be sent if:

* BQ is below 75
* MQ is below 75
* MQ is below 40
* MQ is 40–69
* BQ and MQ are uneven

as long as the existing analysis says the pick is strong/actionable and it has a valid OVER/UNDER direction.

Do NOT assume that a weaker individual metric automatically means the overall pick is bad.

A strong Tier 1 UNDER can have lower MQ.

A strong Tier 1 OVER can have lower BQ.

The overall analysis matters.
