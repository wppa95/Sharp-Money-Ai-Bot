STEP 7 — #116 COMPLETE PROP GRADING
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

This is a major requirement.

The bot should grade the props it has historically tracked, not only props that became Telegram actionable picks.

PROP TRACKING and ACTIONABLE QUALIFICATION are separate systems.

A prop can be:

* Scanned
* Stored
* Tracked
* Not qualified
* Still graded later

The bot must preserve the information needed to determine the eventual outcome.

Desired lifecycle:

PROP SEEN
↓
SNAPSHOT SAVED
↓
LINE HISTORY PRESERVED
↓
SCORING / QUALIFICATION
↓
QUALIFIED OR NOT QUALIFIED
↓
GAME FINISHES
↓
FINAL RESULT FETCHED
↓
HIT / MISS / PUSH
↓
PERSISTENT RECORD
↓
PERFORMANCE / BACKTEST / CALIBRATION

Do NOT make grading dependent on Telegram delivery.

Do NOT require the bot to see the same prop again after the game.

The automated grader must proactively obtain final player results when necessary.

Use the existing /backfill result-fetching pattern as the implementation reference.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
HISTORICAL / PREVIOUS-DAY GRADING
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

The system must be able to grade historical props from previous days.

Example:

A prop was tracked on August 7.

The game finishes.

The bot must be able to find that historical prop on August 8 and resolve it.

☐ Find historical prop
☐ Preserve original line
☐ Preserve historical snapshot
☐ Fetch final player result
☐ Determine HIT/MISS/PUSH
☐ Store result
☐ Keep historical record permanently available

A prop removed from Underdog before or during the event must still be gradeable when a final player result is available.

Do not allow removal of the market to erase the historical record.
