Neither platform offered the UNDER selection in the alert. Neither platform offered a standard line for this stat in the offerings observed by the user.

The bot must never recommend an unavailable selection or misrepresent a Goblin/Demon line as standard.

1. Enforce exact offering validation before queue admission and sending

Trace the existing candidate qualification, durable queue admission, and final delivery paths. Implement the smallest safe general fix.

Before a pick is admitted to the durable delivery queue or sent to Telegram, validate it against the freshest available source offering.

Validation must include:

- Correct originating platform and provider/source identity.
- Correct event identity.
- Correct player identity.
- Correct sport and market/stat identity.
- Exact original line.
- Direction actually offered by that platform.
- PrizePicks line variant/classification, where applicable.
- Current offering availability, freshness, and event eligibility.

A historical hit rate, directional score, cached cross-platform comparison, or another platform's offering must never establish that a proposed selection is currently available.

If source data explicitly shows that the proposed direction is unavailable, reject it with a clear persisted reason such as "DIRECTION_NOT_OFFERED".

If the exact selection cannot be verified from sufficiently fresh source data, fail closed. Do not send it as a confirmed playable pick.

Never:

- Change UNDER to OVER automatically.
- Change the offered line to make a pick qualify.
- Substitute a different platform's line.
- Infer that both directions are available just because a line exists.
- Infer a standard line from a Goblin/Demon offering.

This must be enforced in the actual selection and delivery pipeline, not merely by changing the Telegram message formatter.

2. Standard-line priority — PrizePicks and Underdog

Standard, non-Goblin/non-Demon lines are the primary selection priority.

- Treat standard lines on PrizePicks and Underdog as equal primary options.
- Do not favor one primary platform over the other solely because of its name.
- Correctly identify each platform's actual line and classification.
- A standard line means an actual standard offering from that platform, not a line inferred from another app or from historical data.
- Standard lines must receive priority over otherwise eligible PrizePicks Goblin/Demon alternatives within the same comparable candidate-selection scope.
- Do not let a Goblin/Demon candidate displace a valid standard-line candidate merely because its historical rate, score, or line difference looks more attractive.
- Do not exclude Goblin/Demon offerings globally. They remain secondary options when no suitable standard-line selection is available, subject to exact-offering verification and all existing qualification rules.
- If neither platform offers a standard line for a player/stat/event, do not manufacture one or relabel a Goblin/Demon line as standard.
- In that situation, consider a verified Goblin/Demon selection only as a clearly classified secondary option if it independently passes the existing qualification rules. Otherwise, send no pick for that market.
- If the only offered direction is OVER, no UNDER pick may be sent for that offering, regardless of its classification or historical hit rate.

Apply this consistently to all supported sports, players, markets, and platforms. Do not hardcode Junior Caminero or MLB as a special case.

3. Preserve platform independence and cross-platform comparisons

PrizePicks and Underdog remain equal primary platforms.

Keep the following distinct for each platform:

- Provider/source ID.
- Event ID.
- Player and market identity.
- Original line.
- Offered direction.
- Line classification/variant.
- Source freshness and availability.
- Qualification and delivery records.

Cross-platform comparisons remain informational. They can explain differences between available offerings, but cannot validate a selection that the originating platform does not offer.

Do not suppress a valid standard selection on one platform solely because the other platform lacks that line or direction.

Do not merge Goblin/Demon lines into standard-line identities. Do not let one line, direction, or classification validate a different one.
