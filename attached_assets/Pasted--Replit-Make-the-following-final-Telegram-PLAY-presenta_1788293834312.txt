## 1. PLAY GATE — EVIDENCE/HISTORY IS THE FUNDAMENTAL GATE

IMPORTANT:

Quality is NOT the primary gate blocker for a Telegram PLAY.

The fundamental gate for a PLAY should be valid, sufficient CURRENT-LINE EVIDENCE/HISTORY.

A PLAY should require:

- Valid current Underdog line
- Valid current-line historical game-level evidence
- Sufficient evidence/history to support the evaluation
- Correct current-line hit rates
- Fresh current-line direction/evaluation
- Existing V4 PLAY qualification requirements that are based on evidence/history

Do NOT introduce or preserve a separate Quality-score gate that blocks an otherwise valid evidence-backed PLAY solely because its Quality score is below some arbitrary threshold.

Quality can remain an internal model/evaluation metric and may be used for:
- internal ranking
- analysis
- learning
- reporting
- diagnostics

But Quality must NOT independently block a valid evidence-backed PLAY from Telegram unless that behavior is explicitly required by an existing V4 qualification rule.

Core principle:

EVIDENCE/HISTORY = PLAY GATE

QUALITY = INTERNAL EVALUATION METRIC

Do not invent a new Quality threshold.

Do not change the existing evidence/history requirements.

Do not change the scoring formula.

Do not change the existing direction logic.

Do not change the existing PLAY qualification logic except to remove an unintended Quality-only blocker if one exists.

## 2. REMOVE QUALITY FROM TELEGRAM PLAY DISPLAY

The Telegram PLAY alert is too cluttered.

## 3. REPLACE THE MISLEADING "UNDERDOG PICK" HEADER

The bot uses both Underdog and PropLine/PrizePicks data.

Therefore:

🐶 UNDERDOG PICK

is misleading as the overall alert header because it can imply the analysis is based exclusively on Underdog.

Replace the overall user-facing header with:

📊 SHARP MONEY PLAY

This identifies the message as the bot's actual qualified PLAY.

Still clearly identify the sportsbook/source of the playable line inside the alert.

For example:

📊 SHARP MONEY PLAY
⚾ MLB — Hits + Runs + RBIs
👤 Player

🐶 Underdog: 1.5
Previous: 0.5
Move: +1.0

📌 PICK: UNDER 1.5
💪 STRONG BET

L5: 0/5
L10: 2/10
L20: 8/20
L30: 11/30

Reason: Line is 50% above historical average

The exact available evidence/history should continue to be displayed according to the existing V4 rules.

## 8. LINE MOVEMENT STILL REQUIRES FRESH EVALUATION

Do not weaken the existing line-movement/evidence integrity fixes.

The pipeline remains:

CURRENT LINE
→ CURRENT GAME-LEVEL HISTORY
→ CURRENT EDGE
→ CURRENT DIRECTION
→ CURRENT PLAY EVALUATION
→ PLAY GATE
→ TELEGRAM ONLY IF QUALIFIED

Never reuse old-line evidence.

## 9. MOVEMENT DOES NOT DETERMINE DIRECTION

A line movement is an evaluation trigger, NOT a betting direction.

Examples:

0.5 → 1.5 can produce OVER 1.5 or UNDER 1.5.

1.5 → 0.5 can produce OVER 0.5 or UNDER 0.5.

4.5 → 3.5 can produce OVER 3.5 or UNDER 3.5.

Direction must come from fresh current-line evidence.

Do NOT change the existing direction/scoring model.
