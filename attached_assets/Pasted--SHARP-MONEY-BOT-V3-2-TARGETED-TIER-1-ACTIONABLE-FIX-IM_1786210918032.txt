Problem 1 — “Qualified” is NOT the same as “Actionable”

/funnel “Qualified candidates” comes from:

PropCandidateLog.gate_decision == “ACCEPTED”

The “qualified” state is assigned when:

is_qualified=True

This happens BEFORE the final should_alert decision.

Therefore:

QUALIFIED
≠
ACTIONABLE

A prop can appear as qualified in /funnel while never becoming a Telegram actionable pick.
