4. RESEARCH STRATEGY
==================================================

There may be thousands or tens of thousands of props.

Do NOT make the scan loop wait for an expensive external research request for every prop on every scan.

Instead, build a scalable two-level architecture.

LEVEL 1 — CONTINUOUS STRUCTURED EVIDENCE

Continuously collect and maintain historical/structured evidence for ALL available props.

LEVEL 2 — EXTERNAL INTELLIGENCE

When a prop reaches the actual analyst/research stage, Agent Reach performs a research pass for that prop.

Research prioritization may be used BEFORE a prop reaches the analyst stage based on factors such as:

- significant line movement
- rapid movement
- multiple apps/books moving
- unusual movement
- strong quantitative signal
- significant news potential
- game proximity
- conflicting evidence
- high-value candidates
- candidates approaching alert thresholds
- candidates already being analyzed

However, once a candidate actually reaches the analyst/research stage, Agent Reach research is CORE to that analyst process.
