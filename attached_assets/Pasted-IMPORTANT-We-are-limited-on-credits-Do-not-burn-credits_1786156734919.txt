POST-FREEZE TASK — SEPARATE TELEGRAM PICK GRADING

V1.3 is now frozen.

This is a small post-freeze hardening task.

Currently the bot grades evaluated player props as:

✅ HIT
❌ MISS
🟡 PUSH
⏳ PENDING

That grading infrastructure works.

The missing piece is separating:

ALL EVALUATED PROPS

from:

🎯 TELEGRAM ACTIONABLE BET PICKS

We need to specifically track the results of actionable picks that were actually sent to Telegram.

==================================================
REQUIRED BEHAVIOR

When a 🎯 ACTIONABLE BET PICK is successfully delivered:

Mark/associate that PropOpportunityLog record as:

alert_sent = TRUE

or use an equivalent existing field/relationship if one already exists.

Do NOT create duplicate opportunity records unnecessarily.

Preserve the existing fields:

* player
* stat_type
* line_value
* game_time
* recommendation
* tier
* confidence/bet quality
* provider
* event/game association
* result
* timestamps

==================================================
IMPORTANT DISTINCTION

A prop being evaluated does NOT automatically mean it was a Telegram pick.

Only mark alert_sent when the actionable alert was actually accepted/sent through the Telegram delivery path.

Do NOT mark:

* 📈 MARKET MOVE DETECTED
* PASS
* blocked picks
* rejected picks
* suppressed picks
* internal-only intelligence

as Telegram actionable picks.
