PHASE 7 — REPAIR DIRECTION-AWARE HIT-RATE CALCULATIONS

The "/picks" output appears to use OVER hit rates for UNDER recommendations.

Observed examples:

- Dota 2, zeal, Kills Maps 1+2 UNDER 10.5, results "16, 29, 6, 6, 4", displayed 2/5 (40%). Assuming these are the relevant outcomes and no push, three of five are below 10.5.
- NHL, Liam Ohgren, Goals UNDER 0.5, results "1, 0, 0, 0, 0", displayed 1/5 (20%). Assuming no other result complications, four of five are below 0.5.

Requirements:

1. OVER hit rate = outcomes above the line divided by the eligible resolved sample.
2. UNDER hit rate = outcomes below the line divided by the eligible resolved sample.
3. Pushes must be identified and handled consistently.
4. Voids, cancellations, missing results, and unresolved events must not be counted as ordinary wins or losses.
5. Use one shared calculation implementation across "/picks", "/analyst", "/performance", and any other output showing hit rates.
6. Report numerator, denominator, and sample size clearly.
7. Never report 0% merely because the sample is missing.
8. Test both directions, pushes, integer lines, half-point lines, and empty samples.

PHASE 13 — TESTING AND ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA

Do not declare the work complete just because the bot starts or a command returns a response.

Create or update automated tests for the relevant modules. Use fixtures or mocked provider responses where appropriate. Do not require live bets, paid API calls, or access to production secrets for unit tests.

Minimum acceptance criteria:

A. Provider ingestion

- PropLine ingestion can be tested with a representative real-format response.
- PrizePicks observations are stored with PrizePicks identity and are visible in provider-specific tracking.
- Underdog observations remain separately attributed.
- Failed or unavailable providers produce clear health diagnostics.
- No provider data is relabeled to make counts appear correct.

B. Historical evidence

- Supported history sources return parsed, persisted, matchable game records in tests.
- L5/L10/L20/L30/H2H calculations use the correct player and market.
- Missing history is represented as missing, not as a fake 0% hit rate.
- Unsupported markets are reported honestly.

C. Recommendations

- An eligible candidate creates a canonical recommendation record.
- An ineligible candidate does not become an actionable pick.
- Existing qualification thresholds remain unchanged.
- A recommendation can be traced from source observation to evidence, qualification, alert, delivery, and eventual grading.
- No slips or multi-leg picks are generated.

D. Platform and direction

- The exact platform, player, event, stat, line, and direction are validated.
- Unavailable or unverifiable directions are never falsely advertised as verified.
- PP/UD comparisons use actual provider records.

E. Telegram delivery

- Only qualified individual recommendations are delivered.
- "/analyst" can find the same recommendation records used by delivery.
- Duplicate handling works without suppressing valid new recommendations.
- Failed sends are distinguishable from successful sends.
- The required footer is retained.

F. Grading

- Final result evidence matches the correct event, player, sport, market, and direction.
- OVER, UNDER, push, void, cancellation, and unresolved cases are tested.
- Grading is idempotent.
- Verified outcomes update the corresponding recommendation and analytics records.
- Unverified outcomes remain pending.

G. Analytics and reporting

- UNDER hit rates are direction-aware.
- Provider populations and reporting totals reconcile or have clearly documented scopes.
- "/grades", "/learning", "/rollups", "/performance", and "/backtest" agree on the underlying verified outcomes.
- Dashboard sections "By current sport" and "Recently Tracked Props" are removed.
- The health output reports actual scheduler activity and database writes.

H. Regression protection

- Existing MLB mappings, scoring, thresholds, delivery behavior, and database behavior are protected by regression tests.
- Sport aliases and event-day handling have regression tests.
- Existing working sports and markets are not broken by shared changes.

If the live data providers cannot be contacted in tests, clearly separate mocked test success from live provider verification. Never claim a live integration has been verified when only a mock was tested.
