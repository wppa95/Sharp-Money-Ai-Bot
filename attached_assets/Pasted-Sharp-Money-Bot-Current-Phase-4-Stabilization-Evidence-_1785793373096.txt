Remaining Stabilization Fixes

1) Player Prop Alert Formatting

Current issue:

Alerts show:

OVER → line
UNDER → line

but do not show the actual recommendation.

Fix:

Add:

📌 Suggested Play:
OVER X.X

or

📌 Suggested Play:
UNDER X.X

Use existing direction/selection fields if available.

Do not change scoring.

⸻

2) Align /picks With Qualification Logic

Current issue:

Alert pipeline recognizes:

✅ S
✅ A
✅ qualifying B+

but /picks may only show stored S/A.

Fix:

Make /picks use the same qualification rules.

Show:

✅ S
✅ A
✅ qualifying B+

Hide:

❌ weak B
❌ PASS

Do not change thresholds.

⸻

3) Confidence + Market Quality Accuracy

Current issue:

Example:

Market Quality:
1/4 providers

Confidence:
100/100

Fix:

Market Quality must only count providers with available lines.

Examples:

Only Underdog:
1/1 providers

Underdog + DraftKings:
2/2 providers

Underdog + DraftKings + FanDuel:
3/3 providers

Also:

A single-provider market should not automatically receive maximum confidence.

Make the smallest correction possible.

Do not rebuild confidence scoring.
