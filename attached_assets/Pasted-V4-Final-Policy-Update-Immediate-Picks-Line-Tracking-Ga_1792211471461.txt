V4 Final Policy Update — Immediate Picks, Line Tracking, Game Reminders, and Correct Timezones

Implement the following policy changes in the existing V4 codebase. These requirements supersede conflicting instructions in earlier prompts.

Preserve the completed pipeline repairs, including immediate persisted-batch evaluation, DOTA history-cache hydration, separate LINE CHANGE notifications, source isolation, and deduplication. Do not refactor unrelated components.

1. Game-day eligibility and timezone

Use "America/Chicago" as the bot's configured reporting and display timezone. This is Missouri's Central Time zone and automatically handles daylight saving time.

Required behavior:

- Only send picks for games scheduled on the current calendar day in "America/Chicago".
- Block picks for games scheduled on the next Central Time calendar day until that day begins.
- Do not calculate the reporting day using UTC dates or a hardcoded UTC-5 offset.
- Convert provider event start times from their actual timezone or UTC timestamp into "America/Chicago" before displaying them or making reporting-day decisions.
- If a provider supplies a timestamp with an explicit timezone or UTC offset, respect it. Do not assume a naive timestamp is already Central Time without verifying the provider's convention.
- Preserve started-event protection, pregame checks, line freshness, source identity, valid selectable sides, existing qualification gates, and deduplication.

2. Send qualified picks immediately

A qualified pick for a game scheduled on the current Central Time reporting day must be sent as soon as it becomes available.

- Remove any arbitrary two-hour-before-game restriction that delays or blocks the initial pick.
- Do not wait for later provider requests, another sport's feed, or a reminder timer before delivering a qualified pick.
- Start tracking the pick and its verified line immediately so subsequent line movements can be detected.
- Keep PrizePicks and Underdog as equal primary platforms with their own source IDs, lines, event identities, histories, and delivery records.
- Send individual picks only. Do not create or send two-man or three-man slips.

3. Add a separate two-hour game reminder

The initial pick and the two-hour reminder are two separate notifications with different purposes.

Initial pick:

- Send immediately when the pick qualifies.
- Begin line tracking immediately.
- Do not delay the initial pick to synchronize with the reminder.

Reminder:

- Schedule a separate reminder for two hours before the actual game start.
- Calculate the reminder time from the verified event timestamp converted correctly into "America/Chicago".
- Display the game start and reminder times in Central Time.
- Associate the reminder with the original pick, platform, event, player, market, and verified selection.
- Do not create a second betting pick, alter the original pick, or count the reminder as a new qualified pick.
- Deduplicate reminders so scheduler restarts or repeated evaluations cannot send duplicates.
- Cancel or update a pending reminder when the associated event or selection is no longer valid, using the existing safety and lifecycle rules.
- If a pick first qualifies less than two hours before game time, send the pick immediately. Do not delay it or send a misleading late two-hour reminder. Record that the scheduled reminder time has already passed.
- Do not send reminders after the game has started.
- Use the existing scheduler and database architecture where possible. Avoid unnecessary new scheduler jobs, overlapping tasks, or unbounded queues.

Requirements:

- Avoid double-converting timestamps that are already timezone-aware.
- Verify the conventions for each affected provider or sport rather than assuming all feeds format times identically.
- If an event timestamp is missing or ambiguous and cannot be safely resolved, do not fabricate a start time or schedule an unreliable reminder. Log the issue and apply existing safety behavior.
- Display the correct Central Time start on the original pick and its reminder.

5. History requirements — latest policy

Remove the previously requested stricter MLB and NFL historical-window requirements.

MLB and NFL:

- Require more than five actual historical games for the historical-evidence portion of qualification.
- Six real historical games or more meets this minimum; five or fewer does not.
- Do not require every L5/L10/L20/L30/H2H or six-window hit-rate calculation to be populated.
- Missing L20/L30 windows must not automatically reject a pick when the minimum historical-game requirement is met.
- Preserve existing unrelated qualification thresholds and safety checks.

NBA, WNBA, NHL, and all other supported sports:

- History may be limited or unavailable.
- Do not impose a universal five-game or six-window minimum.
- A pick may qualify without historical results if a valid, supported OVER or UNDER direction can be established through the existing permitted evaluation logic.
- Missing history must not manufacture a direction or automatically authorize a pick.
- Continue enforcing source integrity, selectable-line validation, freshness, event-state checks, existing qualification rules, and deduplication.
- Use L5/L10/L20/L30/H2H, average, median, and context when actually available. Clearly preserve unavailable windows as unavailable rather than fabricating values.
- Do not lower unrelated qualification thresholds simply to increase alert volume.

Ensure the same intended history policy is applied to PrizePicks and Underdog.
