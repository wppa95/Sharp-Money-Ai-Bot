1. CORE REPORTING RULE

The bot’s reporting must distinguish the entire persisted prop universe from directional opportunities and Telegram delivery.

A prop being non-actionable or not delivered does NOT mean it was not tracked.

The authoritative reporting population must come from the persistent provider-separated prop observation/history data.

Do NOT use Telegram delivery counts as the definition of tracking.

3. ACTIVE TRACKING VS DIRECTIONAL VS DELIVERY

Keep these populations separate:

Tracked

Every persisted eligible prop observation/snapshot that belongs to the active/current tracking universe.

Directional

Tracked props for which the model produced a valid OVER or UNDER direction.

Delivered

Directional opportunities actually sent to Telegram.

Pending

Tracked/directional records whose event has not yet produced a final result.

Resolved

Records with a final result available.

HIT / MISS / PUSH

Resolved directional outcomes based on the stored line and direction.

Do NOT collapse these populations into one number.

A prop can be:

Tracked → Directional → Pending → Resolved → HIT/MISS/PUSH

without ever being delivered to Telegram.

4. EVERY PROP REMAINS GRADEABLE

Every persisted monitored prop should remain eligible for eventual grading when:

* its event completes
* the actual result becomes available
* the prop has sufficient information to grade

This applies regardless of:

* provider
* sport
* whether it moved
* whether it was actionable
* whether it was delivered
* whether it was previously rejected
* whether it was previously rescanned
* whether it had evidence
* whether it was cold-start

Do NOT restrict grading to Telegram-delivered picks.
