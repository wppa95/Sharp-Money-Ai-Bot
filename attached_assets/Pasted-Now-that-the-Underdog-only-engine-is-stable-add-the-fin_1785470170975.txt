Now that the Underdog-only engine is stable, add the final intelligence layer for prop selection.

Do not expand sportsbook functionality. Keep DraftKings/FanDuel/ML/totals disabled but preserve code.

Goal:
Turn the Underdog engine from a line-movement detector into a true prop selection system that prioritizes high-probability “green goblin” style plays.

Implement only the following:

1. Add Market Quality / Softness Score

Create a market quality layer that evaluates how efficient or inefficient a prop market is.

Consider:
- Market liquidity
- Historical movement frequency
- Prop type difficulty
- Sample size
- Stability of outcomes

Boost priority for:
- MLB Hits
- MLB Hits + Runs + RBIs
- MLB Fantasy Score
- WNBA Points/Rebounds/Assists/PRA
- Tennis stable volume markets
- Soccer volume markets
- Other proven high-floor markets

Penalize unless elite evidence supports:
- HR 0.5
- RBI 0.5
- Stolen Bases 0.5
- Low-frequency outcome props

Do not remove markets. Adjust confidence/priority only.

2. Add Market Pressure Flag

Create a warning layer based on market behavior.

Detect:
- Fast line movement
- Multiple line updates
- Large movement
- Market disappearing/removal risk

Display as a warning only.

Do NOT increase confidence because of market pressure.
Do NOT create alerts based only on movement.

3. Add Smart Movement Re-Evaluation

When a meaningful Underdog line change occurs:

Recalculate the full prop evaluation.

Triggers:
- New prop
- Significant line movement
- Player/status change if available
- Matchup change if available

Ignore:
- Duplicate snapshots
- Tiny meaningless changes

The flow should be:

Line change detected →
Refresh evidence →
Recalculate score →
Apply market quality →
Apply variance →
Apply qualification rules →
Alert only if qualified.

4. Preserve current Underdog improvements

Do not modify:
- Historical Activity calibration
- Drift velocity adjustment
- Current evidence breakdown
- S/A/B/PASS thresholds
- Alert qualification thresholds

Unless tests prove a change is required.

4A. Prevent over-filtering / allow elite no-movement opportunities

The engine should not require line movement as the primary reason for an alert.

Allow qualified alerts from:
- New Underdog props
- Existing props with no meaningful movement
- Line movement events

ONLY when the full evidence profile supports it.

For high-floor markets, allow the model to prioritize:
- Strong hit rate
- Strong consistency
- Median outcome edge
- Stable role/usage
- Low variance

Preferred markets:
- MLB Hits
- MLB Hits + Runs + RBIs
- MLB Fantasy Score
- WNBA Points/Rebounds/Assists/PRA
- Tennis volume markets
- Soccer volume markets

Do NOT loosen standards for:
- HR 0.5
- RBI 0.5
- Stolen Bases 0.5
- Low-frequency outcome props

Do not lower S/A/B/PASS thresholds globally.

Goal:
Avoid a situation where stacking multiple filters creates zero alerts. The engine should produce selective but realistic opportunities.
