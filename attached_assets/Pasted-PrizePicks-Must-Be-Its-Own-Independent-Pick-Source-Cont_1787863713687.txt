PrizePicks Must Be Its Own Independent Pick Source

Continue from the completed PropLine PrizePicks integration.

I need to clarify the intended architecture:

PrizePicks is NOT merely a confirmation/reference source.

PrizePicks should operate as its own independent betting source alongside Underdog.

The bot should be capable of generating and delivering:

* Underdog Pick
* PrizePicks Pick

so I immediately know which platform to place the bet on.

1. PRIZEPICKS AS AN INDEPENDENT SOURCE

Treat PrizePicks through PropLine as a first-class source, similar to Underdog.

PrizePicks observations should:

* Enter the shared normalized prop universe.
* Be independently evaluated.
* Be independently eligible for actionable-pick delivery.
* Preserve PrizePicks as the source/provider identity.
* Maintain their own current line and direction.
* Be tracked independently for historical performance.
* Be graded independently when results become available.

Do NOT require a matching Underdog prop for a PrizePicks Pick to become actionable.

Do NOT require PrizePicks to merely confirm an Underdog prop.

Both sources should be capable of producing their own picks.

3. SAME PROP ON BOTH PLATFORMS

If the same player/market appears on both Underdog and PrizePicks:

Do NOT merge them into one platform.

Instead:

* Keep the Underdog observation.
* Keep the PrizePicks observation.
* Evaluate each source’s actual line independently.
* Preserve each source’s line, direction, timestamp, and provider identity.

If both independently qualify, Telegram may show:

UNDERDOG PICK
and

PRIZEPICKS PICK

as separate actionable picks.

If only one qualifies, send only that source’s pick.

4. CROSS-PLATFORM INFORMATION

The bot may use the other platform’s information as supporting market context where appropriate.

For example:

Underdog line = 24.5
PrizePicks line = 22.5

The system may recognize that the difference between platforms is meaningful information.

However:

* Do not overwrite one provider’s line with another provider’s line.
* Do not change the source identity.
* Do not fabricate a line.
* Do not turn a PrizePicks observation into an Underdog pick.
* Do not turn an Underdog observation into a PrizePicks pick.

Each actionable pick must remain tied to the actual platform/source.
