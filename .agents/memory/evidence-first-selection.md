---
name: Evidence-first candidate selection
description: The delivery selector must rank already-qualified candidates without a sport or market allowlist.
---

The strongest-pick selector is a ranking and deduplication layer, not a second qualification policy. Once a candidate has passed the canonical direction and completed-history gates, do not apply a sport/stat “green goblin” allowlist or low-line preference that can remove it.

**Why:** The green-goblin filter discarded valid MLB/NFL candidates after they had real completed-game evidence, making the monitoring cycle report many qualified evaluations but no Telegram deliveries.

**How to apply:** Keep evidence and direction checks at the canonical collection/delivery gates. Let the selector choose among qualified candidates using provider/player identity and evidence ranking only.
