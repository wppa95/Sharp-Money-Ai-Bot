## Rule

Market movement alone cannot create a directional betting recommendation.
Missing optional player-result history must not block an otherwise qualified
direction; MLB/NFL alone require at least six verified actual games, not full
historical windows.
Never fabricate result windows, rates, evidence, or a direction from a move.
Factual LINE CHANGE notifications are separate from independently qualified picks.

Active no-direction evaluations are represented as `None` at the decision and
Underdog candidate boundaries. Legacy `PASS` values may remain in historical
records and compatibility constructors, but must not be generated by the active
decision path or treated as a current qualification state.

The canonical delivery boundary independently requires `OVER`/`UNDER` and the
sport's existing safety requirements. Evidence detectors may accept real history, explicit
supporting validation, or a populated evidence/intelligence trace, but ordinary
decision metadata is not evidence.

Direct compatibility betting broadcasters must also preserve typed line
freshness, provider validation, pregame timing, provider-owned identity,
deduplication, and claim release. Generic Telegram transport remains available
for operational notices such as health or quota warnings; those messages are
not betting-alert paths and must not be forced through prop qualification.

`/picks` is a separate current directional research view. It requires a current
provider-owned row, a safely known pregame state, and `OVER`/`UNDER`, but not
Telegram delivery or canonical evidence. Resolve direction before provider-aware
deduplication and fair result limiting so non-directional rows or provider order
cannot hide valid current opportunities.

Keep `/picks` reads bounded from the requested display limit. Reconcile
Underdog overrides by exact source identity only, and constrain optional
intelligence to selected candidates; broad sport/history grouping can exhaust
the command's three-second deadline. Nested report cancellation must propagate
to child database tasks.

**Why:** A market-only bypass turned line movement into an actionable alert without
player-result support, violating the separation between monitoring and betting
recommendations.

**How to apply:** Keep evaluating, persisting observations, and rescanning
evidence-missing props. Gate all direct and queued delivery paths through the
shared direction-and-evidence check, then apply provider, freshness, timing,
identity, deduplication, claim/release, and rate safeguards at the delivery
boundary. Preserve genuine provider event time/status on current rows and keep
unknown state excluded. Keep non-betting operational broadcasts explicitly
separate. For `/picks`, bound current-row overfetch before reconciliation while
applying the final display cap only after effective direction resolution.
