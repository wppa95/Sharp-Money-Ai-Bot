NFL and CFB are the only football-family competitions allowed for Telegram. Accept NCAAF and explicit football_ncaaf provider spellings only as aliases of the active CFB scope; other gridiron, rugby, and Australian-rules aliases remain blocked even with otherwise valid evidence. Supported non-football sports—including soccer, FIFA, CS2 (canonical CS), LoL, WNBA/NBA, UFC, VAL(ORANT), and Dota—remain eligible only when the provider/feed has a valid market and all delivery gates pass. KBO/NPB have no verified current player-result/feed mapping and are outside the active scope; do not enable them by guessing. Eligible picks still require an explicit OVER or UNDER direction plus sufficient evidence/history; primary game results and explicitly settled PropLine results use the same five-sample minimum. Stars, score, confidence, Bet Quality, Market Quality, and S/A/B/C tier remain internal/reporting metadata and must not be rendered in Telegram delivery. Existing safety, live-game, deduplication, freshness, scope, rate-limit, and validity protections remain operational safeguards.

**Why:** The user confirmed only NFL and active CFB are acceptable football-family sports, and their current list names NCAAF as the provider alias for CFB. Prevent other football families from leaking into Telegram without suppressing supported non-football sports.

**How to apply:** Enforce the shared football-family policy in candidate selection and the final Telegram gate; allow NFL/CFB and explicit NCAAF aliases of CFB only. Keep supported non-football sports eligible only with verified canonical provider/catalog mappings, and preserve direction, completed-history source/sample, timing, freshness, tracking, deduplication, and explicit operator-disable checks. Preserve tier and quality values for ranking, logs, database records, and diagnostics, but strip them from Telegram-facing alert text. Render STRONG BET for either supported direction, and never use the Telegram gate to stop underlying evaluation.
