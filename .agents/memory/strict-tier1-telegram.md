---
name: Telegram sport scope
description: Football delivery exclusions and canonical evidence requirements for actionable alerts.
---

The monitoring and evaluation pipeline continues ingesting and tracking every supported sport. Telegram delivery allows NFL and CFB, blocks other football-family sports (including NCAAF, CFL, and FCS), and keeps MLB plus non-football sports eligible. NFL/CFB may use completed primary game results or explicitly settled PropLine results under the same five-sample minimum as other eligible sports; they are not blocked merely for being gridiron. Eligible player picks still require an explicit OVER or UNDER direction plus sufficient evidence/history; stars, score, confidence, Bet Quality, Market Quality, and S/A/B/C tier remain internal/reporting metadata and must not be rendered in Telegram delivery. Existing safety, live-game, deduplication, freshness, scope, rate-limit, and validity protections remain operational safeguards.

**Why:** The user explicitly requires NFL and CFB to be allowed while other football-family sports remain blocked from Telegram. MLB and all non-football sports should alert when backed by legitimate evidence. Quality metadata must not silently suppress otherwise valid eligible-sport picks.

**How to apply:** Use the disallowed-football predicate for NCAAF/CFL/FCS/AFL/rugby and similar aliases; allow only NFL and CFB through ingestion, history, grading, and Telegram delivery. Keep MLB enabled. Preserve tier and quality values for ranking, logs, database records, and diagnostics, but strip them from Telegram-facing alert text. For settled-result evidence, require every L5/L10/L20/L30/season window to be at ≥70% OVER rate or ≤40% OVER rate for UNDER before delivery. Render STRONG BET for either supported direction, and never use the Telegram gate to stop underlying evaluation.
