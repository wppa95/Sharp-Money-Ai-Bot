---
name: Strong pick quality gate
description: The quality boundary shared by player-prop alerts and the /picks command.
---

MLB and NFL require more than five verified actual historical games: six is
sufficient, five is not. Do not require complete historical windows or the
former six-window hit-rate cutoffs. All other supported sports, including NBA,
WNBA and NHL, have optional history and require a valid supported OVER/UNDER
direction plus the existing unrelated qualification and safety checks.

**Why:** The user's final policy explicitly replaced the older main-league
six-window rule and universal historical sample requirements.

**How to apply:** Apply this split equally to PrizePicks and Underdog throughout
active qualification and delivery. Do not fabricate missing windows or direction,
lower unrelated direction thresholds, cap otherwise eligible individual picks,
or turn movement into a pick. Retain raw observations and grading evidence.
