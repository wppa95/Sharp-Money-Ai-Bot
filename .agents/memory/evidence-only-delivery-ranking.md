---
name: Evidence-only delivery ranking
description: Player-prop delivery ordering must use settled directional hit-rate evidence, never confidence or quality labels.
---

Within each sport's delivery queue, directional delivery ranking prioritizes the settled season hit rate: higher OVER-perspective rate for OVER picks and lower rate for UNDER picks. A 70% OVER and 30% UNDER therefore have equal 0.70 directional strength. Evidence/sample availability may break ties; confidence, tiers, quality labels, and market-movement bonuses must not.

**Why:** The user wants sports ranked independently and expects season-level 70% OVER / 30% UNDER evidence to be recognized as strong.

**How to apply:** Use season hit rate first for ordering within a sport, fall back to existing directional history when season data is unavailable, and never compare candidates across sports.
