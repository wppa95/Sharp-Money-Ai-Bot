NFL, NBA, and MLB Telegram delivery are restricted to strong, directional picks from the existing high-floor “green goblin” stat categories; OVER requires directional confidence ≥75 and UNDER requires directional confidence ≤25, then the lowest available qualifying line wins within each provider/player group.

**Why:** The user wants NFL, NBA, and MLB alerts limited to repeatable markets, the easiest available line, and an explicit strong-bet direction rather than volatile, higher, weak, or neutral selections.

**How to apply:** Keep this policy in the shared strongest-pick selector so live, standing, and full-pool delivery paths use the same filter. Do not change selection rules for other sports.
