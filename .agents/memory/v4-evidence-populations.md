---
name: V4 evidence and reporting populations
description: Boundaries between completed-game evidence, provider observations, and Telegram performance reporting.
---

Completed player game results are the only evidence that can qualify a directional Telegram PLAY or populate L5/L10/L20/L30 hit-rate displays. Each named window requires its full sample size; provider polling snapshots are monitoring context, not games.

**Why:** Treating line observations as games inflated history and allowed an apparently supported pick to reach delivery without actual player performance evidence.

**How to apply:** Keep proxy/provider observations available for monitoring and scoring context, but exclude them from delivery evidence and user-facing hit-rate history. Reports must label delivery-independent historical grading separately from records marked as delivered Telegram PLAYs.
