---
name: Comprehensive market discovery
description: Discovery scope must be independent of qualification and preserve distinct source offerings.
---

Discover and account for every player, market, and distinct offered line
exposed by existing sources across all supported sports. Discovery is not
limited by preferred markets, historical alert volume, grading rank, or
qualification. Preserve source-specific lines, directions, raw market identity,
and standard/special classifications only when supplied.

**Why:** The user explicitly requires comprehensive scanning while retaining
selective, evidence-based individual alerts; missing alerts must not be
mistaken for missing source markets.

**How to apply:** Trace observation, normalization, storage, evaluation,
history, qualification, and delivery separately. Count unique offerings
separately from repeated observations. Mark unobserved coverage unknown, not
unavailable or successfully scanned. Do not weaken qualification, bypass quota
or cooldown safeguards, add providers, or use line differences to create picks.
Pushes are not UNDER wins; requested rolling windows use valid completed
results and strict directional counts.
