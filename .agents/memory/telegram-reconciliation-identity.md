---
name: Telegram reconciliation identity
description: Durable identity requirements for reconciling outbound Underdog Telegram sends with stored picks.
---

Delivery audits must retain provider source identity and originating path; player/stat/line alone is not a deterministic join key. Set `alert_sent` only from a confirmed successful ACTIONABLE_PICK audit matched to the exact provider, source, player, sport, event, market, original line, and direction; PrizePicks also requires its line variant and a matching current PropLine source row. Before sending queued work, revalidate both the latest source observation and current directional opportunity. A historical alert view may preserve an audit's original line and side after the mutable opportunity row changes, but only when provider, source, event, player, sport, and market still match; display and grade against the audit's original line, never today's line.

**Why:** A historical send can be proven successful at the Telegram API layer while remaining impossible to join to the exact opportunity when source identity is omitted or multiple lines/events share a label. Historical presentation must survive a changed line without making that old audit proof that the current line was delivered. Directly setting the legacy flag can claim delivery without an exact successful audit; delayed queue work can also outlive its qualified source or direction.

**How to apply:** Keep current-delivery gates and reconciliation exact across line and direction. Historical alert history may join a complete audit to the same provider/source/event/player/sport/market while retaining the audit's own line/direction. Use identity-matched successful audits—not the compatibility flag—for delivery reports. Do not repair historical flags from transport logs alone; distinguish API sends from tracked opportunity rows.
