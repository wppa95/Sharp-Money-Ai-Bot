The Terms define API keys and require calls to include the applicable key; they distinguish development/personal and production usage. The Valorant policy page includes registration, game-integrity, tournament-policy and production-key/RSO sections. These pages establish that “public documentation” is not equivalent to permission for this bot's production use. Exact current quota and approval outcome must be confirmed in the Riot developer account/product process.
