# Oracle's Elixir policy caveat

**URL:** https://developer.riotgames.com/policies/general  
**Linked from:** https://oracleselixir.com/tools/downloads  
**Fetched:** 2026-09-26  
**Tier:** 1 (Riot first-party policy, linked by the data publisher)

Oracle's Elixir's downloads page explicitly links Riot Games' general terms and policies and says game statistics are Riot's property. This is important evidence that a public CSV/Drive file is not automatically licensed for a bot. The precise permitted-use terms must be read at the time of deployment and confirmed for the intended product; this research does not infer a license from public visibility.
